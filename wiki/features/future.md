# Future Development & Roadmap

Planned features and technical evolutions for the Panini World Cup 2026 Album Tracker.

## Phase 2: Backend & Persistence
- **Cloud Sync**: Move from `localStorage` to a centralized database (PostgreSQL/DynamoDB).
- **Authentication**: Secure login with JWT or AWS Cognito.
- **Real-time Collaboration**: Ability to share "live" progress with friends.

## Phase 3: Community & Social
- **Trading System**: Feature to identify trade opportunities with other users based on duplicates.
- **Global Leaderboards**: See who is closest to finishing the album globally.
- **Verified Completion**: Proof-of-work/completion badges.

## Phase 4: Advanced Utilities
- **OCR Scanner**: Use the camera to scan stickers and automatically update the album.
- **Notification System**: Reminders or alerts for new features/trading opportunities.
- **PWA Enhancement**: Full offline capabilities and push notifications.
